Why Your Body Needs Excersise

A growing body needs regular physical activity to build strong muscles and bones, keep the heart healthy, manage body weight, and support brain development for better focus and mood. Moving every day helps children grow up strong and full of energy.

Quick Health Tips
  • Eat fresh fruits and crunchy vegetables every day.
  • Choose whole grains like brown rice or roti.
  • Pick water instead of sweet sodas or packaged juices.
  • Stop eating when you feel full.
